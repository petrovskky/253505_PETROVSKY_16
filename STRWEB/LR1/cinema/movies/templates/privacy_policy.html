4. Exercise of the rights of personal data subjects

4.1. The personal data subject has the right to:
4.1.1. to receive information regarding the processing of his/her personal data, except as provided for by law. The list of information and the procedure for obtaining it are established by the Law on Personal Data;
Within 5 business days after receiving an application from the personal data subject, the operator provides the relevant information or notifies him of the reasons for refusing to provide such information.
4.1.2. require the Operator to clarify his/her personal data if the personal data are incomplete, outdated, inaccurate;
Upon his/her application containing the relevant requirements, the personal data subject shall attach the relevant documents and (or) duly certified copies confirming the need to make changes to the personal data. In case of confirmation of the fact of inaccuracy of personal data, the Operator, based on the information provided by the personal data subject, clarifies (changes) personal data within 15 days from the date of submission of such information.
4.1.3. to receive information about the provision of his/her personal data to third parties, once a calendar year free of charge;
Within 15 days after receiving the request, the operator will provide the personal data subject with information about what personal data of the subject and to whom were provided during the year preceding the date of filing the application, or notify the reasons for refusal to provide it.
4.1.4. withdraw his/her consent to the processing of personal data at any time without giving reasons;
The right is exercised in accordance with the procedure for submitting an application to the Operator in accordance with Article 10 of the Law on Personal Data, or in the form through which its consent is obtained.
The Operator, within 15 days after receiving the application in accordance with its content, will stop processing personal data, as well as delete them (ensure the termination of the processing of personal data, as well as their deletion by authorized persons, if any) and notify the User thereof.
If the Operator does not have the technical ability to delete personal data, the Operator will take measures to prevent further processing of personal data, including their blocking, and will notify the personal data subject thereof within the same period.
The Operator has the right to refuse to satisfy the requirements of the personal data subject to terminate the processing of his personal data and (or) their deletion if there are grounds for the processing of personal data provided for by law.
4.1.5. appeal against the actions (inaction) and decisions of the Operator that violate its rights in the processing of personal data to the authorized body for the protection of the rights of personal data subjects in the manner prescribed by the legislation on appeals of citizens and legal entities.
4.2. In order to exercise the rights specified in clause 4.1. of this Policy, the personal data subject shall submit an application to the Operator.
The application must contain:
- surname, first name, patronymic (if any) of the personal data subject, address of his/her place of residence (place of stay);
- date of birth of the personal data subject;
- identification number of the subject of personal data, in the absence of such a number - the number of the document,verifying the identity of the personal data subject, in cases where this information was indicated by the personal data subject when giving his consent to the operator or the processing of personal data is carried out without the consent of the personal data subject;
- a statement of the essence of the personal data subject's claims;
- personal signature or electronic digital signature of the personal data subject.
The application may be sent in writing, in the form of an electronic document signed with an electronic digital signature in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Belarus.
If the application of the personal data subject does not reflect all the necessary information in accordance with the requirements of the Law on Personal Data or the subject does not have the right to access the requested information, then a reasoned refusal is sent to him, as well as in other cases in accordance with the law.
4.3. For assistance in the exercise of rights, the personal data subject may also contact the person responsible for internal control over the processing of personal data in the Organization by sending a message to the e-mail address: info@starlight.by.
